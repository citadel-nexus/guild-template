# Guild Constitution

Status: draft. Replace each prompt and obtain human ratification before granting powers.
The machine counterpart is `governance/guild.yaml`; keep both representations in review.

## Identity

PROMPT: State the guild identifier, legal owner and accountable human steward.

## Purpose

PROMPT: State the enduring mission and explicit success criteria.

## Jurisdiction

PROMPT: List domains, tenants and environments within the guild boundary.

## Reserved Powers

PROMPT: Identify powers retained by humans or other guilds.

## Prohibited Powers

PROMPT: List actions the guild must never take, even when requested by a parent.

## Parent Children

PROMPT: Declare organizational relationships separately from mutation authority.

## Human Authority

PROMPT: Name human approvers, override limits and review requirements.

## Agent Authority

PROMPT: Map approved capabilities to A1-A9 and define when actions remain HELD.

## Delegation

PROMPT: Define scoped, expiring, revocable delegations and approval evidence.

## Escalation

PROMPT: Define conflict, appeal, timeout and unavailable-approver handling.

## Truth Requirements

PROMPT: Distinguish planned, configured, deployed and observed claims; require evidence.

## Amendment Procedure

PROMPT: Require a scoped SRS, dispatch, diff review, tests and human ratification.

## Succession

PROMPT: Specify how authority transfers when a steward or guild is unavailable.

## Dissolution

PROMPT: Specify who may dissolve the guild and how data and obligations transfer.
